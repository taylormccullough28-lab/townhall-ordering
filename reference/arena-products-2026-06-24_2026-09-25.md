## Five things to act on

### 1. Two invoices, 8 days apart, are line-for-line identical - $5,781.06

| | Invoice A | Invoice B |
|---|---|---|
| Order id | 2051056 | 2056684 |
| Invoice | OH0002811019 | OH0002821151 |
| Date | 2026-07-22 (Wed) | 2026-07-30 (Thu) |
| Total | **$5,781.06** | **$5,781.06** |
| Lines | 30 | 29 |

**All 29 of B's line items match A exactly** - same codes, same quantities, same
line prices. A carries one extra $20 `Liquor` line; B books the same $20 as a
delivery charge, so the totals land identically.

Twenty-nine lines agreeing on both quantity and price is not something two
genuine orders do. Either the same delivery was invoiced twice, or Arena
re-keyed the order. **Worth $5,781.06 to ask Gursev about.**

### 4. MarginEdge collapses 1L and 750ml into one product, so unit counts mix sizes

Arena's codes end in `L` (1 litre) or `B` (750ml), and MarginEdge maps both to a
single product. Product-level quantity therefore adds different-sized bottles:

| Product | Reads as | Actually |
|---|---|---|
| Tito's Handmade Vodka | 597 units | 444 x 1L + 153 x 750ml |
| Absolut Vodka | 354 units | 348 x 1L + 6 x 750ml |
| Casamigos Reposado | 63 units | 39 x 1L + 24 x 750ml |
| Grey Goose | 65 units | 61 x 1L + 4 x 750ml |
| Espolon Blanco | 37 units | 30 x 1L + 7 x 750ml |

**35% of Arena spend sits on products split this way.** For the ordering engine
this is the difference between 444 litres and 597 litres of Tito's - a 25% error
on the single largest line in the building. Depletion has to be computed per
vendor item code, never per product name.

One pair looks wrong rather than merely collapsed: **Dewar's White** is `0202B`
at $49.82 and `0202L` at $26.32. Every other B/L pair has the 750ml *cheaper*.
Either `0202B` is a larger format than its code suggests or the price is wrong -
worth a look, though it is only $152.28 of spend.

### 5. Names and account refs are inconsistent enough to break a search

- **`8523228242` is booked as "GIELARD PAMPLEMOUSE"** - that is Giffard
  Pamplemousse, 12 bottles at $26.99 on 2026-09-17. Searching the catalogue for
  "Giffard" does not find it. Three other Giffard products *are* spelled
  correctly (`Giffard Rhubarb`, `Giffard Orgeat Syrup 1L`, `Giffard Abricot Du

- "Oyo Honey Vailla 750ml" (`7699B`) - Vanilla.
