In the glass

Total pour7.50 oz

Build

  1. Pack a wine glass with ice.
  2. Pour the Spritz Biz over the ice.
  3. Top with Prosecco.
  4. Stir once with a bar spoon — lift from the bottom, don't whip the bubbles out.
