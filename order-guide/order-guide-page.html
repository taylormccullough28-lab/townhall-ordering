Before you send it

  • Colostrum — the recipe asks for 100 g against a 96 g jar, which forces a second jar. Rounding to 96 g saves $54.95 a batch.
  • Peanut butter — 1,875 g against an 1,814 g bag, same problem. Rounding to 1,814 g saves a bag.
  • Flax — Margin Edge says 135 g per batch, the slide says 35 g. This list uses 35 g.
  • Chia — Margin Edge shows Costco at $8.79, well under the Amazon price here. Confirm the source before ordering.
  • Matcha — Margin Edge's Being Brigid uses a culinary grade at a sixth the price of the Santé ceremonial listed here.
  • Three items have no photo — cocoa powder, allulose and cashew butter were identified through Margin Edge rather than a screenshot.
