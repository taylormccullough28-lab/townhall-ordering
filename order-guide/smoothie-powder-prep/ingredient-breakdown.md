## 5. Shared prep method (identical on all four slides)

All four batches use the same procedure — worth stating once in the final guide and referencing
per recipe rather than repeating it four times:

1. Ensure the measuring container and batch container (large mixing bowl) are completely dry.
2. Scale all ingredients separately and place them in the large dry mixing bowl.
3. Using a piano whisk, whisk all ingredients until thoroughly combined — approx. 20–30 seconds.
4. Using a fine mesh strainer, sift the ingredients until thoroughly combined.
5. Place into a sealed airtight container and shake for 30 seconds.
6. Place the finished mixture into the designated, clean, labeled, dry storage container with silica
   packets. Any remaining product goes into an airtight container with silica packets for storage.

> Note: the Strawberry and Keto slides spell out steps 1–2 ("ensure containers dry", "scale
> separately"); the Longevity and Being Brigid slides skip straight to the whisk step. Same process —
> the final guide should carry the full 6 steps for all four.
