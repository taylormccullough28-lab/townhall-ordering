## Recommended Actions

1. **Pre-shift script, non-negotiable:** beer list, cocktail list, special menu, and one beverage favorite — named out loud at every table, every shift. Four questions, 11 points, missed 16 out of 16 times.
2. **Dessert and coffee at plate-clear.** It is the single biggest question on the survey at 10 points. Avery proved it works — have her walk the team through how she does it.
3. **Bone broth and bulletproof coffee belong to the server.** On 09/17 the manager made the offer and the server still scored zero. The table's server owns it.
4. **Bar ticket times.** Three of four shops waited over 2 minutes for an alcoholic beverage. Look at bar staffing and ticket routing at the open of lunch and dinner.
5. **Café app ask — every transaction, both halves.** Ask *and* give the benefit. Nila is the model; Emma needs training, not correction.
6. **Phone standards:** name the restaurant, name yourself, ask before holding, know today's specials. **Marcia's 09/27 call is the training tape.** Two of four hostesses did not know the specials when asked.
7. **Manager visibility.** No manager was observed at all on 09/14 or 09/27. On 09/26 the manager never touched a guest table. On 09/17 the manager was in the middle of everything — and that was our second-highest score of the month.
