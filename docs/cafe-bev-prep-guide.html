Read before you batch

1. The two batch names are swapped in the prep doc

Helena's prep sheet pairs each mixture name with the other one's ingredient. Her build sheet, sent the same day, has the pairing right.

  • Prep sheet: "COCONUT BLUE SPIRULINA MIXTURE: 1 liter reverse osmosis water + 1 tablespoon of chlorophyll"
  • Prep sheet: "CHLOROPHYLL WATER MIXTURE: 1 liter reverse osmosis water + 4.5 grams of organic blue spirulina"
  • Build sheet: "chlorophyll mixture (chlorophyll and reverse osmosis water)" · "Coconut blue spirulina mixture (coconut water + blue spirulina)"

This guide uses the corrected pairing: chlorophyll goes in the chlorophyll mixture, spirulina goes in the spirulina mixture. Batch it that way, but get Helena's yes in writing before rollout.

2. Coconut water or RO water in the spirulina mixture?

The build sheet calls it "coconut water + blue spirulina." The prep sheet says 1 liter RO water + 4.5 g spirulina. The drink name says coconut, and Spirulina Reviver already tops off with coconut water on top of the mixture — so the two sheets point different directions on cost and flavor.

Her own Spring/Summer 2026 cost sheet points the same way: the Alter Ego cocktail card lists the coconut blue spirulina mixture with coconut water as its ingredient line, not RO water.

Coconut water is the likely answer, but it's still her call. Batch RO water for training only; don't lock a par or a cost sheet until she confirms.

3. Use the 1.5 lb ginger yield, not the 3 lb

Helena sent the prep sheet twice on Aug 1. The 17:14 version says 3 lbs of peeled ginger; the 17:20 resend — subject line "FIXED INGREDIENT ON GINGER JUICE USE THIS ONE" — says 1.5 lbs. Everything else is identical.

1.5 lbs is current. Delete the earlier printout from the station.

Batch mixtures

Prep first · every drink depends on these

Three components get batched ahead. Nothing else on the menu can be built until they're done, so these lead the prep list.

Chlorophyll Water Mixture

Body of Chlorophyll Vitality
Batch · 1 L

Yields 1 liter

34 fl ozReverse osmosis water (1 L)
1 TbspNature's Way Chlorofresh liquid chlorophyll
  1. Measure 1 liter of RO water into a clean, labeled container.
  2. Add 1 tablespoon of chlorophyll.
  3. Stir thoroughly to combine — chlorophyll settles, so it has to fully disperse.
This mixture is the whole body of the drink, roughly 4–5 oz per 12 oz glass once it's packed with ice. One liter covers about 6–7 drinks. It's the first batch you'll run out of on a busy morning — count it in pars accordingly.

Coconut Blue Spirulina Mixture

Used in Iced Blue Coconut Matcha & Spirulina Reviver
Batch · 1 L

Yields 1 liter

34 fl ozReverse osmosis water (1 L) — or coconut water, see conflict 2
4.5 gMicroIngredients organic blue spirulina
  1. Measure 1 liter of water into a clean, labeled container.
  2. Scale 4.5 grams of blue spirulina — weigh it, don't spoon it. This is a color and cost control point.
  3. Stir thoroughly to combine until there are no streaks or clumps left.
This one goes a long way: 0.50 oz per matcha and 0.75 oz per Reviver means a liter covers roughly 45+ drinks. Batch a half liter until we know real volume, so we're not dumping product.

Ginger Juice

Used in Chlorophyll Vitality
Batch · 1.5 lb

Per batch

1.5 lbPeeled ginger
to coverWater — enough to fully cover the ginger
  1. Peel 1.5 lbs of ginger and add it to the blender.
  2. Add enough water to cover the top of the ginger completely.
  3. Blend 30 seconds on high.
  4. Strain through a chinois into a labeled container.
Helena's sheet doesn't give a finished yield. Measure the strained juice on the first three batches and write it on the container — that's how we set a real ginger par instead of guessing.

Prep-shift checklist

House standard — not in Helena's docs

Helena's sheets cover recipes, not handling. This is our side of it — confirm the hold times against our house cold-prep standard before it goes on the wall.

  • Batch the chlorophyll mixture first. It empties fastest and every Vitality is blocked without it.
  • Weigh the spirulina on a gram scale. Eyeballing 4.5 g swings the color and the cost.
  • Peel, blend, strain ginger — then record the yield on the container so we can build a real par.
  • Date and initial every container the moment it's filled, including the batch size.
  • Fresh ginger juice gets the shortest life of the three batches. Set its discard day against house standard and don't carry it past.
  • Stir the chlorophyll and spirulina mixtures before each service — both settle out while they sit.
  • Coconut water stays chilled and rotated. It's the fill on three drinks, so warm product shows up in every one of them.
  • Check the ice well before open. Every one of these builds is spec'd packed with ice; short ice means a short pour and a wrong drink.

Fall prep recipes — draft for approval

Not Helena's spec · needs a tasting before it trains

One of the three is real: the pumpkin spice allulose syrup is already built and scaled in the recipe system, and its card below is the live recipe, not a draft. The other two syrups have nothing behind them, so those cards are a starting point to be tasted and corrected — every quantity in them is a first pass, and neither goes on the wall until Helena or Erik signs it.

The drafts are anchored on what the built recipe and Helena's other work already establish: her café builds use a 12 oz glass, her cocktail cards portion flavor modifiers at 0.5–1 oz, and the house seasoning ratios run about 0.9% pink Himalayan salt and 2.75% vanilla bean paste on the same two products the keto syrup uses.

Pumpkin Date Syrup

Draft · for Pumpkin Date Latte
Draft · ~1 qt

Yields about 32 oz

8 ozPitted Medjool dates
8 ozOrganic pumpkin puree
4 ozUnrefined coconut sugar
1 TbspPumpkin spice
24 ozHot water
  1. Soak the dates in the hot water for 15 minutes to soften.
  2. Add dates, soaking water, pumpkin puree, coconut sugar, and pumpkin spice to the blender.
  3. Blend 60 seconds on high until completely smooth — no date skin or fiber left.
  4. Strain through a chinois. Date skins will clog a pump if they get through.
  5. Cool, then date, initial, and label.
Pumpkin spice is in this draft because Helena's Aug 28 ingredient list has it, even though the printed menu copy dropped it. Confirm which is right before batching. Dates and coconut sugar make this the sweetest of the four — taste it against the Honeybun Latte before setting the pour.

Salted Maple Vanilla Bean Syrup

Draft · for Salted Maple Vanilla Bean Latte
Draft · ~24 oz

Yields about 24 oz

16 ozOrganic pure maple syrup
8 ozWater
30 gSimply Organic vanilla bean paste
10 gPink Himalayan salt
  1. Warm the maple and water gently — warm to the touch, never boiling. Boiling maple turns it bitter.
  2. Whisk in the salt until fully dissolved.
  3. Whisk in the vanilla bean paste until no streaks remain.
  4. Cool, then date, initial, and label.
The salt is the whole drink — it's in the name, and it's what separates this from a maple latte. Weigh it, don't spoon it. Salt and vanilla here are scaled off the pumpkin spice allulose recipe, which runs 0.92% salt and 2.75% vanilla bean paste — same two house products, so the seasoning lands in a familiar range.
