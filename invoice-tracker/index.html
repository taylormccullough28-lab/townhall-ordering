How a vendor gets flagged. A vendor that billed in at least 6 of the last 8 weeks is expected every week: no invoice this week marks it Missing, and fewer than half its usual count marks it Low. A vendor that billed in 4 or 5 of the last 8 weeks shows Check when nothing came in. Vendors that bill less often than that are listed under occasional vendors.

Weeks run Monday to Sunday by invoice date. An invoice uploaded late still lands in the week it was dated, so refresh after uploads catch up. Marking a vendor "No delivery" or "Found" clears it for everyone who opens this page.
