# Game Day Expectations

**Game day. Read this before you clock in.**

This is one of the biggest days of the year for us. Everyone is going to make money. That only happens if we execute at 100% of our standards, 100% of the time, at 100% volume.

## Getting here

- **Be on time.** On time means in the building, in full uniform, ready to work — not pulling into a spot.
- **Plan for parking now.** Traffic and lots will be a mess. Leave early enough that parking is not your excuse. If you are late because you left at your normal time, that is on you.

## Non-negotiables

- **No drinking.** Not before, not during, not on a break. Zero tolerance.
- **No discounts.**
- **No comps.**
- **No comp promo bottles.**
- **Check every ID.** Every single one. We do not lose our license on a football Saturday.

## Uniform

- **Body suits are $25** — that is your discounted price off the retail cost.
- **Every body suit and polo must be rung in before you receive it.** No exceptions, no "I'll get it later."
- **You will not receive an OSU body suit or polo unless you are working the game tomorrow.**

## On the floor

- **Every server carries a wine key.** Have it on you before your first table.
- **Get a manager for any issue.** Any issue. **If you need security, say it clearly and say it early** — be direct about what you need and we will handle the situation. You will never get in trouble for calling for help. You will get in trouble for not calling for help.
- **If you leave the floor for any reason, tell a manager you are leaving the floor.** Every time.

## Primary, secondary, tertiary

- **Primary job** — your guests are satisfied.
- **Secondary job** — find things to do. If you see something on the floor, pick it up. If you walk past the restroom and it needs attention, tidy it while you are in there.
- **Tertiary job** — help other staff.

There is always something to do, but the guests get taken care of first.

## Getting out

- **Nobody walks out alone.** Make sure somebody walks you out. If you are leaving and no one is with you, find a manager.

Take care of each other, take care of the guests, and go get it.
