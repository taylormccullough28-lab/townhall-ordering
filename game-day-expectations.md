# Game Day Expectations

**GAME DAY. Read this before you clock in.**

Everyone is going to make money today. That happens when we execute at 100%.

- **Be on time.** In the building, in full uniform, ready to work. **Plan your parking now** — traffic will be a mess. Parking is not an excuse.
- **No drinking. No discounts. No comps. No comp promo bottles.** Zero tolerance.
- **Check every ID. Every single one.**
- **Body suits and polos are $25, and you get your discount off that $25.** It must be rung in before you receive it. You do not get an OSU body suit or polo unless you are working the game tomorrow.
- **Every server carries a wine key.**
- **Get a manager for any issue.** If you need security, say it clearly and say it early — we will handle it. You will never get in trouble for asking for help. You will get in trouble for not asking.
- **Leaving the floor for any reason? Tell a manager.** Every time.
- **Primary job** — your guests are satisfied. **Secondary** — find things to do: something on the floor, pick it up; restroom needs attention, tidy it. **Tertiary** — help other staff. Guests come first.
- **Nobody walks out alone.** Make sure somebody walks you out.

Take care of each other, take care of the guests, and go get it.
