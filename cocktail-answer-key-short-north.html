Section 2

What gets sent back

18 points

Two points each. Award the full two if they have the substance — the wording will not match.

1. Which drink on this menu is never shaken, and what happens if you shake it?Scarlett Spritz. It is built in the glass. Shaking flattens the Prosecco.
2. Where does the Pernod go on a Green Goddess, and at what point in the build?In the glass, not the tin. Rinse and dump the excess before you shake.
3. When is the soda added to a No New Friends, and why never in the tin?After the shake, in the glass. Shaking soda kills the bubbles and can blow the tin apart.
4. Which two drinks are served up, and what does that mean for the glass?Chai Hard and Espresso Martini. Chilled coupe, no ice in the glass.
5. What must you do before you start building an Espresso Martini?Ask the guest which liquor they want. It is the only spec with no liquor written into it.
6. In what order do the two LMF 2.0 garnishes go on, and why does it matter?Dragonfruit powder first, then the calendula. Dusting over the flowers buries them.
7. Why do you strain over fresh ice instead of the ice you shook with?Shaken ice is chipped and already melting; it waters the drink down in the glass.
8. Which drink has no fixed total pour, and why?No New Friends. The soda tops the glass rather than being measured.
9. A guest orders a drink and you are out of the biz. What do you do?Tell the guest it is unavailable and call it to the bar lead. Never substitute or free-pour a replacement.

Specs change. Re-print this key whenever the build sheet changes, and re-certify the bar team.
